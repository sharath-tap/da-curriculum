Every set has the same difficulty mix, so no student gets an easier interview than another. Model answers and solutions included — you do not need to know the subject to run this.

Start here — before question 1

“Tell me about yourself.” Two minutes. Do not interrupt. You are listening for whether they can structure an answer, not for the content.

“Take me through one project from your resume.” Ask what their specific contribution was. If they say “we” throughout, ask what they personally wrote.

Then move to the ten questions below.

SQL

Now open the laptop

Two hands-on tasks. Let them type it themselves in a notebook. Give them a few minutes each — you are watching how they approach it, not only whether it runs.
