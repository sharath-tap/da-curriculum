Your two sources, and what each is for

Statistics appears in neither of your Python playlists. It exists in exactly two places, and they are good at different things.

Source one

Data Science playlist — videos 070 to 076

Seven videos, about 10 hours. Same organisation, same teaching style, same 90–110 minute rhythm as your own classes.

  • Use it for probability, the normal distribution, hypothesis testing, and the A/B example
  • Use it to learn delivery — pacing, repetition, which analogies land with this cohort
  • Warning: videos 070, 071 and 072 are titled “Probability” but are actually normal distribution and hypothesis testing

Source two

Udemy — sections 5 to 8

42 lectures, about 9 hours 43 minutes. Tightly structured, short lectures, covers everything the playlist misses.

  • Use it for foundations, all of descriptive statistics, distributions, CLT, confidence intervals and the tests
  • Use it for structure — the section order is close to the order you will teach
  • Warning: §7 has four distribution lectures you can skip — listed at the bottom of this page
